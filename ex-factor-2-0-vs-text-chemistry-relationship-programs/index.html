Who Created These Relationship Programs?

Creator names help identify the offer, but they are not the same thing as independent proof of effectiveness or professional credentials.

Ex Factor 2.0

Ex Factor 2.0 creator: Brad Browning

The official Ex Factor 2.0 presentation identifies Brad Browning and frames the product as a breakup and reconnection guide. The offer is presented as a larger package: a 163-page interactive ebook, a 20-part video course, a three-part video series, and additional ebooks.

The practical takeaway is simple: evaluate the format and the situation it addresses, not the strongest promise on the sales page. If you are looking for help after a breakup, the package is at least organized around that question. It still cannot guarantee that an ex will reply, return, or choose to rebuild the relationship.

Text Chemistry

Text Chemistry creator: Amy North

The official Text Chemistry presentation identifies Amy North and frames the product around texting, attraction, and communication. Its public offer describes 13 videos plus The Phone Game, Why Men Leave, and Tinder Success Secrets ebooks.

That narrower focus can be useful when the question is what to say, when to reply, or how to think about a confusing message. It is less obviously suited to someone who wants a complete breakup-recovery plan. As with any self-help guide, treat outcome language as marketing rather than a promise about another person.
