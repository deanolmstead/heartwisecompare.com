The short verdict

Start with the situation, not the slogan.

These offers are adjacent, but they are not interchangeable. One begins with a troubled marriage; the other begins with dating, attraction, and a seller-authored explanation of men.

Our read

It depends on the chapter you are in.

Choose the marriage-repair system if the relationship itself is the immediate focus. Choose the dating guide if the need is understanding a dating situation or the seller's communication framework.

The most useful difference is the intended starting point.

Save The Marriage System presents a package of reports, a Quick-Start Guide, and audio reports for readers focused on a marriage that needs attention. Its checkout page states that the offer carries a 60-day, 100% money-back guarantee.

That's Not How Men Work presents instant access to a guide plus bonus material for readers interested in meeting and dating men. Its checkout page separately describes an optional Mastery Series with recurring billing, so that optional offer deserves a careful look before acceptance. The same page states an eight-week, 100% money-back guarantee.

Neither product can control another person's choices. The comparison is about the structure and stated focus of each offer, not a promise that either one will produce a particular romantic outcome.
