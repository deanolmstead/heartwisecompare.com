About the byline

Who is Jordan Wells?

A transparent byline is more useful than implying expertise that has not been verified.

Jordan Wells is the editorial byline used by Heartwise Compare for source-led comparison work. The byline represents a small editorial desk focused on reading current seller presentations, recording the package details a buyer can inspect, and separating product facts from marketing language.

Jordan Wells is not presented as a therapist, counselor, clinician, or relationship researcher. The byline makes the page accountable without suggesting professional credentials that are not part of this site’s evidence. When a question calls for clinical, legal, crisis, or safety support, a digital self-help guide is not a substitute for qualified local help.

Our process

How we build a comparison.

Each page follows the same practical sequence so readers can see where the recommendation comes from.

01

Read the current seller presentation

We start with the public product page and record the name, stated creator, format, listed materials, access language, refund language, and any obvious checkout caveats.

02

Separate a product detail from a promise

“13 videos” or “instant access” is a package detail. A promise that a person will return, commit, or change is a marketing claim. We do not present that outcome as a fact.

03

Match the guide to the reader’s situation

A breakup guide, texting guide, and marriage-repair guide may all use relationship language, but they are not interchangeable. The recommendation starts with the reader’s actual decision.

04

Disclose affiliate relationships

Some product buttons use affiliate links. If a reader buys through one, Heartwise Compare may earn a commission at no extra cost to the reader. That possibility does not determine the editorial verdict.

05

Date the research

Seller pages, prices, packages, support details, and refund terms can change. Each comparison includes a checked date so readers know when its visible details were reviewed.

Reader safeguards

What we will not promise.

Good comparison content should make a decision clearer without pretending it can control another person or remove uncertainty.

No guaranteed romantic outcome

A course can offer ideas, examples, or a structure for reflection. It cannot guarantee another person’s interest, consent, forgiveness, commitment, or behavior.

No substitute for professional help

These pages cover self-help digital products. Abuse, coercion, stalking, threats, crisis, and serious mental-health concerns require qualified support rather than a product recommendation.
