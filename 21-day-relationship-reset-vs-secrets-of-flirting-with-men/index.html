Frequently asked

Questions worth answering plainly.

A few direct answers before you open either offer, including format, recurring terms, and the limits of relationship advice.

Which program is more structured day to day?

The 21-Day Relationship Reset is the clearer fit because the seller organizes its guide and workbook around a three-week sequence with daily exercises and reflections.

Which program focuses more directly on flirting and dating conversations?

Secrets of Flirting With Men is the more direct fit because its seller page emphasizes formulas, example phrases, body language, and flirting in different situations.

Can either program guarantee attraction or commitment?

No. Educational material may offer ideas or practice prompts, but it cannot control another person’s feelings, boundaries, consent, or decisions.

Should I use either program instead of therapy or safety support?

No. The 21-Day Relationship Reset explicitly says it is not therapy, and neither program should replace qualified support for abuse, crisis, serious distress, or safety concerns.
