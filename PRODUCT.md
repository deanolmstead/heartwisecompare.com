# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People, primarily women, comparing online relationship guides and other digital self-help products. They are often scanning on mobile and want the practical package, price, format, checkout, and fit details before deciding whether to continue to a seller page.

## Product Purpose

Heartwise Compare is an independent, source-led comparison site. It separates verifiable package details from seller promises, makes recurring checkout terms visible, and helps readers choose based on format and fit rather than guaranteed romantic outcomes. Success means a reader can understand the meaningful differences quickly, spot important caveats, and make a more informed next step.

## Brand Personality

Clear, grounded, humane. The voice should feel like a careful editor who respects the reader's agency, names uncertainty plainly, and never turns relationship anxiety into pressure.

## Anti-references

Avoid manipulative relationship-marketing pages, guaranteed-outcome claims, breathless urgency, testimonial theater, hidden recurring offers, and generic affiliate-site patterns that bury the useful comparison under decorative persuasion.

## Design Principles

- Lead with the decision a reader is trying to make.
- Put price, format, recurring terms, and limitations where they can be scanned.
- Treat seller claims as claims and distinguish them from observable package details.
- Keep safety, consent, and reader agency visible without sounding clinical or alarmist.
- Make the site feel editorial and composed, but never let polish obscure the evidence.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA fundamentals: readable contrast, keyboard-visible focus, semantic headings and landmarks, labeled controls, usable mobile layouts, reduced-motion support, and no information conveyed by color alone.
