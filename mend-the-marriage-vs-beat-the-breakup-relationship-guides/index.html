Which sounds more like you?

Start with the problem you want to solve.

One is about trying to keep a marriage together. The other is about getting your life back after a breakup.

Pick Mend the Marriage if

You and your spouse are still together, even if things feel difficult, and you want a structured place to start.

Not a match if: the relationship is over or you need therapy or couples counseling right now.

Pick Beat the Breakup if

The relationship has ended and you want help calming the mental loop, finding closure, and getting your footing back.

Not a match if: you are actively trying to repair the marriage or need immediate mental health support.

A quick reality check: A guide can give you ideas, but it cannot make a spouse stay or make heartbreak disappear overnight. For abuse or immediate safety concerns, look for qualified local support.

Which guide is for an ongoing marriage?

Mend the Marriage is the better match if you are still married and want help working on the relationship. Its page lists a 270-page interactive ebook, a 4-hour audio course, a 7-part video series, and worksheets.

Which guide is for moving on after a breakup?

Can either guide guarantee that a relationship will be saved?

No. A guide can offer ideas, but it cannot promise that a spouse will stay, change, forgive, or return.

No. These are self-help guides, not therapy or counseling. If there is abuse, pressure, stalking, or immediate danger, look for qualified local support.
