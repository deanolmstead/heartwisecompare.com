The short answer

No program can win someone back.

Frequently asked

Questions worth answering plainly.

Direct answers about guarantees, the top two picks, the outliers, and what to verify before you buy.

Can any of these programs guarantee I will get my ex back?

No. Every program on this list is a set of advice materials — ebooks, videos, message templates — not a mechanism that controls another person's choices. Treat reconciliation promises, overnight timelines, and “make them obsessed” language as sales copy. What you can genuinely evaluate is format, package depth, and whether the program fits your situation.

What is the difference between Ex Factor 2.0 and Text Your Ex Back?

Ex Factor 2.0 is the larger multimedia package: a 163-page interactive ebook, a 20-part video course, a three-part video series, and three additional ebooks, organized broadly around breakup reversal. Text Your Ex Back is narrower and texting-specific: a phased system of named text sequences with video, audio, and ebook training plus lifetime community access. Choose breadth of material or texting focus.

Why are Beat the Breakup and Avoidant Recovery on a get-your-ex-back list?

Because they are the honest outliers. Beat the Breakup is about letting go and moving forward after a breakup, not reopening the relationship. Avoidant Recovery is about healing after an anxious–avoidant dynamic — identity, boundaries, and emotional safety. If you are unsure reconnection is right for you, read those pages before buying a reconnection program.

What should I check before buying any of these?

Confirm the current package contents, whether anything recurs or auto-renews, and the refund language on the final order page — details change after our research date. And if your situation involves abuse, coercion, stalking, or serious distress, a program is not the right help: reach a qualified professional or a local support service instead.
