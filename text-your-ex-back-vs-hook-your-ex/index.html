The short answer

Choose the learning format—not a promised reaction.

Start with Text Your Ex Back when you want a structured multi-format program with separate guidance tracks. Choose Hook Your Ex when you want a compact set of ready-to-adapt message templates. Pause on both if the appeal depends on guaranteed reconciliation: no set of texts can override another person's boundaries or decisions.

Decision guide

Start with your situation.

These products organize relationship advice differently. Neither can guarantee another person’s attention, consent, or commitment.

Choose Text Your Ex Back when

You want a fuller program with video, audio, and written material, and you prefer working through a phased system rather than isolated templates.

Choose Hook Your Ex when

You want something compact you can read and apply the same day, and you prefer adapting named templates to your own voice.

Pause on both when

You are looking for guaranteed reconciliation, a way around another person's boundaries, or support for abuse, coercion, stalking, crisis, or serious distress.

Frequently asked

Questions worth answering plainly.

A few direct answers before you open either offer, including format, recurring terms, and the limits of relationship advice.

Which program has more material?

Text Your Ex Back is the larger package, with an ebook plus video and audio training modules. Hook Your Ex is positioned as a compact course built around message templates.

Which is better for someone who prefers reading?

Both offer text versions of their presentations. Text Your Ex Back includes an ebook as part of its core package, while Hook Your Ex publishes a full text version of its pitch.

Do either of these products include recurring charges?

Neither product's core offer was presented as a subscription when reviewed, but always recheck the current order form for preselected upsells or recurring offers before paying.

Can either program guarantee my ex will come back?

No. A messaging guide can organize ideas and examples, but it cannot control another person's feelings, consent, boundaries, or decisions.
