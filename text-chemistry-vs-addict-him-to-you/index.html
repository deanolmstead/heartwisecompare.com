Frequently asked

Questions worth answering plainly.

A few direct answers before you open either offer, including format, recurring terms, and the limits of relationship advice.

Which program is more focused on texting?

Text Chemistry. It is built around twelve named text-message types for dating, re-attracting, and commitment situations. Addict Him to You is a PDF e-book on attraction psychology that also covers re-attracting a man, but texting is not its organizing method.

Which program has more material?

Text Chemistry names more moving parts: twelve message techniques plus three bonus e-books. Addict Him to You is one main e-book with three bonus reports and a newsletter series in its members' area.

Do either of these products include recurring charges?

Neither product's core offer was presented as a subscription when reviewed, but always recheck the current order form for preselected upsells or recurring offers before paying.

Can either program make him obsessed with me?

No. Obsession and guaranteed-love claims are sales copy. A guide can suggest wording and framing, but it cannot control another person's feelings, consent, boundaries, or decisions.
