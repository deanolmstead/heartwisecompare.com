Which one fits?

Use the format as the tie-breaker.

A small price difference should not matter more than whether you will actually use the material.

Start with The Woman Men Adore if…

You want a five-part sequence and prefer a broad relationship framework. Pause at checkout and decline the Adore Club trial unless you intentionally want a $39 monthly membership after 30 days.

Start with Infatuation Scripts if…

You want video, audio, and ebook options plus clearly named bonuses. Pause at checkout and decline the Ultimate Attraction Series unless you intentionally want a $37 monthly charge after the free month.

A relationship guide has hard limits.

Both sellers use language about triggering attraction, creating infatuation, or changing how a man responds. Those are marketing claims—not outcomes Heartwise Compare can verify.

Healthy relationships require mutual consent, honest communication, respect, and room for either person to say no. A commercial guide cannot guarantee another person's feelings, choices, exclusivity, or commitment.

If a situation involves abuse, coercion, stalking, threats, crisis, or serious mental-health concerns, a digital self-help product is not a substitute for qualified local support.

Before checkout

Three questions worth answering.

Check the current checkout rather than relying only on a sales video's headline.

Which program costs less?

The reviewed prices were $47 for The Woman Men Adore and $49.95 for Infatuation Scripts. That $2.95 difference is smaller than the difference between their optional monthly add-ons.

Are the monthly programs required?

The reviewed order pages presented them as optional. The Woman Men Adore showed a 30-day Adore Club trial followed by $39/month. Infatuation Scripts showed a free first month of the Ultimate Attraction Series followed by $37/month. Confirm the boxes and total before submitting any order.

Can either program make someone commit?

No. A guide can offer ideas, examples, or reflection prompts. It cannot control another person's attraction, consent, feelings, communication, or commitment.
