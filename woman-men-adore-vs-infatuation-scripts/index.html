The short answer

Choose the learning format—not a promised reaction.

Start with The Woman Men Adore when you want a single written sequence and prompts you can revisit. Choose Infatuation Scripts when multimedia lessons and concrete examples help you learn. Pause on both if the appeal depends on triggering a guaranteed reaction: neither product can create consent, attraction, or commitment.

Decision guide

Start with your situation.

Choose The Woman Men Adore when

You want a calmer written sequence, prefer reflection over memorized lines, and expect to revisit the same framework over time.

Choose Infatuation Scripts when

You learn better through video and audio and want examples that can be adapted rather than copied mechanically.

Pause on both when

You are looking for guaranteed commitment, a way around another person's boundaries, or support for abuse, coercion, crisis, or serious distress.
