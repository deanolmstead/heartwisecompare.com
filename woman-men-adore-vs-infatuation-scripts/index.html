The short answer

Choose the learning format—not a promised reaction.

Start with The Woman Men Adore when you want a single written sequence — five named modules from a licensed counselor — that you can reread and work through slowly. Choose Infatuation Scripts when you learn by watching, listening, and borrowing exact wording: it is the more scripted, multimedia option. Both carry 60-day ClickBank guarantees, and both try to attach a recurring extra at checkout, so the order form deserves as much attention as the sales page. Pause on both if the appeal depends on triggering a guaranteed reaction: neither product can create consent, attraction, or commitment.

Decision guide

Start with your situation.

These products organize relationship advice differently. Neither can guarantee another person’s attention, consent, or commitment.

Choose The Woman Men Adore when

You want a calmer written sequence, prefer reflection over memorized lines, and expect to revisit the same framework over time. It also fits if you want guidance split by situation — dating or married — from a counselor rather than a coach.

Choose Infatuation Scripts when

You learn better through video and audio and want word-for-word examples that can be adapted rather than copied mechanically. It also fits if a members area you can dip into on any device suits you better than a book.

Pause on both when

You are looking for guaranteed commitment, a way around another person's boundaries, or support for abuse, coercion, crisis, or serious distress. Pause, too, if either sales page's outcome language is doing the persuading — judge the packages, not the promises.

Package details

Two different presentation styles.

Concrete materials matter more than the emotional promises surrounding them.

Five-module written relationship guide

The Woman Men Adore

A long-form written program by licensed professional counselor Bob Grant, framed for both single and married women and organized into five modules built around his 'Campfire Effect' — the idea that emotional presence, not constant giving, is what holds a man's attention. His name for the trap the program teaches against is the 'Giving Pit': doing more and more for a man until he takes you for granted.

By: Bob Grant, P.L.C.

What the seller lists

  • Module 1: The 1 Critical Emotion Men Need to Fall in Love — why hiding your feelings can read as coldness, and the signals that make a woman seem desperate
  • Module 2: His Deepest Desire in a Woman — how to express feelings so a man actually listens, and why arguing is framed as too weak a tool
  • Module 3: What to Expect in the Coming Days — the transformation process, including a word-for-word three-sentence technique
  • Module 4: Simple Changes That Trigger Arousal in a Man — behavior and communication changes, including his 'reflective listening' technique
  • Module 5: Your Dream Comes True — He'll Cherish You Always — keeping attraction and appreciation alive in an established relationship
  • Two situation bonuses: a guide for single women applying the program to dating, and a guide for wives and girlfriends on reactivating a faded spark
  • A free 30-day trial of the Adore Club, with video instruction and members-only forums (see the checkout note — it becomes a recurring membership)

Limits to keep in view

  • The official page uses emotionally loaded outcome language — 'irresistible desire,' 'he'll cherish you always' — that should be treated as marketing.
  • The Campfire Effect is the author's own framework from his counseling practice, not an independently tested method.
  • No guide can guarantee a partner's behavior, attention, or commitment.
  • The club trial requires a deliberate decision: it was preselected at checkout when we checked (September 8, 2026) and continued on a recurring basis unless declined or canceled.
The core program is a one-time purchase. The bundled 30-day Adore Club trial becomes a recurring membership afterward unless declined or canceled.

Video, audio, ebook, and example scripts

Infatuation Scripts

A multimedia package by dating coach Clayton Max, built around one idea — the 'Infatuation Instinct' — and the word-for-word scripts meant to trigger it. Max presents himself as a coach with an applied-psychology background and more than a decade of experience; the program is sold as the system he teaches private clients.

By: Clayton Max / UniCa Publications

What the seller lists

  • Scripts organized by trigger: Curiosity, Investment, and Uncertainty scripts form the core of the method
  • Named script sets, including Independence, Intrigue, Cliffhanger, Barrier, Curveball, Shaping, Temptation, 'Interested-but-not-sold,' and Urgency scripts
  • The same material in video, audio, and ebook formats, with members-area access emailed after ordering
  • Bonus: The Commitment Calculator (Carlos Cavallo) — an interview arguing that most men are not commitment-phobic, and naming the signals that make a man pull back
  • Bonus: Make Any Man Yours For Life (Amy North) — a cheat-proofing guide covering why men cheat, the warning signs, and prevention steps
  • Bonus: Why Men Shut Women Out (Slade Shaw) — a mini-book drawn from an anonymous survey of more than 700 men
  • The bonus set has changed since this page was first published; the three above are what the seller's current presentation and order form list.

Limits to keep in view

  • Scripts may feel unnatural when copied without adapting them to your own voice.
  • The Infatuation Instinct is the author's framing; the sales page supports it with research citations, but it is not an independently verified mechanism.
  • No wording can override another person's boundaries or guarantee a response.
  • Which edition you pick decides the recurring question: the VIP Edition bundles the series trial, the Silver Edition leaves it out entirely.

The guarantee, in plain terms.

Both programs are sold through ClickBank, which matters more than the sellers' own promises. Here is how the protection behind both offers actually works.

How refunds work

60 days, no questions asked

Both sellers state the same terms: a 60-day, 100% money-back guarantee. You request the refund within 60 days of purchase — through the seller's support email or through ClickBank — and you don't have to persuade the author or justify the request.

What shows up, what to check

ClickBank billing, instant access

ClickBank is the retailer for both, so the charge on your statement is a ClickBank charge rather than the program's name — discreet, and the name to look for if you're checking a statement. Access is instant for both: The Woman Men Adore walks you through setting up an account after ordering, and Infatuation Scripts emails a members-area login. The guarantee covers the core programs; the recurring extras described above run until you cancel them, so read the final order form before you confirm.

A necessary limit

A relationship guide has hard limits.

Neither package can guarantee attraction, commitment, reconciliation, or a particular response. Scripts should never replace honest communication, respect for boundaries, or professional support when safety, coercion, abuse, or serious distress is involved.

Frequently asked

Questions worth answering plainly.

A few direct answers before you open either offer, including format, recurring terms, and the limits of relationship advice.

Which guide is better for women who prefer reading?

The Woman Men Adore is the clearer fit because its core package is organized as a five-module written framework. The modules are paginated and written to be reread, and the author is a licensed counselor rather than a coach.

Which product offers more media formats?

Infatuation Scripts lists video, audio, and ebook versions of the main material, plus three bonuses: The Commitment Calculator, Make Any Man Yours For Life, and Why Men Shut Women Out.

Do either of these products include recurring charges?

The core programs are one-time purchases, but both checkouts attach a recurring extra. The Woman Men Adore bundles a free 30-day Adore Club trial that becomes a recurring membership unless declined or canceled; it was preselected when we checked the order form on September 8, 2026. Infatuation Scripts sells two editions: the VIP Edition includes a free first month of the Ultimate Attraction Transformation Series and then bills monthly until you cancel, while the Silver Edition excludes it. Recheck the current order form before paying.

Can either guide guarantee commitment or attraction?

No. A self-help guide can organize ideas and examples, but it cannot control another person's feelings, consent, boundaries, or decisions.

Why don't you list prices?

ClickBank sellers change prices and run promotions, so a price printed here could be wrong by the time you read it. The official checkout page always shows the current price before you pay.

Is either one a subscription?

The programs themselves are not. Both base programs are one-time purchases; the subscription risk sits in the extras — the Adore Club trial bundled with The Woman Men Adore, and the Ultimate Attraction Transformation Series trial bundled with Infatuation Scripts' VIP Edition. Each continues on a recurring basis after its free period unless you decline or cancel.

How do refunds actually work?

Both are ClickBank products whose sellers state a 60-day, no-questions-asked money-back guarantee. Within 60 days of purchase, email the seller's support address or contact ClickBank for a full refund; you don't have to explain yourself to the author.

What shows on my card statement?

A ClickBank charge. ClickBank is the retailer for both programs, so the statement line shows ClickBank rather than the program's name — the name to look for if you are checking a statement.

How do I get the program after paying?

Immediately. The Woman Men Adore walks you through setting up an account for the written program, and Infatuation Scripts emails you a members-area login for the video, audio, and ebook materials.
