The short answer

Choose the learning format—not a promised reaction.

Choose Capture His Heart when you are dating and want a video-based program on attracting a committed partner. Choose Save My Marriage Today when your marriage is in crisis and you want a structured written course on changing your own responses. Pause on both if the appeal depends on guaranteed love or a saved marriage: no program can control another person's feelings, consent, or decisions.

Decision guide

Start with your situation.

These products organize relationship advice differently. Neither can guarantee another person’s attention, consent, or commitment.

Choose Capture His Heart when

You are dating or looking for a committed relationship, you prefer learning by video, and you want named techniques plus checklists for choosing and attracting a partner.

Choose Save My Marriage Today when

Your marriage is in trouble or on the verge of separation, you prefer reading and written exercises, and you want guidance focused on your own behavior and responses rather than changing your partner.

Pause on both when

The situation involves abuse, coercion, or safety concerns. Neither program is a substitute for professional counseling or support services, and no guide can guarantee another person's decisions.

Frequently asked

Questions worth answering plainly.

A few direct answers before you open either offer, including format, recurring terms, and the limits of relationship advice.

Which program is for dating, and which is for marriage?

Capture His Heart is aimed at women who are dating and want to attract a committed partner. Save My Marriage Today is aimed at married women whose relationship is in crisis or near separation. They address different relationship stages.

How is each program delivered?

Capture His Heart is an online video training program with worksheets and checklists. Save My Marriage Today is a written home-study course of e-books and lessons with instant download access.

Are there recurring charges to watch for?

Both sellers present the core program as a one-time purchase through ClickBank. Save My Marriage Today's order form also offers an optional trial membership, so check that optional boxes are unchecked if you do not want it. Confirm the final order form before paying.

Can either program guarantee he will stay or commit?

No. Both sellers market strong promises, but no program can control another person's feelings, consent, or decisions. Save My Marriage Today's own materials recommend face-to-face consultation with a registered professional for serious difficulties.
