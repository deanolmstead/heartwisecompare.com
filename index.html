Fit check

Pick the program that fits how you learn.

Do not choose based only on a big promise. Choose based on the topics and lesson style, and what you can check before you buy.

Pick His Secret Obsession if

You want to study the Hero Instinct and Secret Signals ideas. You also like examples about texts, attention, and a relationship that feels less close than before.

Skip it if: you need a full lesson list, want neutral dating advice, or need help from a trained expert.

Pick The Devotion System if

You want several ways to learn. Its page lists an ebook, 13 videos, a quiz, and bonus books. That makes it easier to see what you may get.

Skip it if: you dislike strong "obsession" claims or expect a course to control another person's feelings.

One more check

Neither program can replace clear boundaries, honest talk, or a safe plan. A course can give you ideas. It cannot make another person say yes, talk to you, commit, or stay.

FAQ

Questions before you buy.

These short answers use the details shown on the seller pages.

Are these digital products?

Yes. Both are digital self-help products. Check the seller page for the current name, price, and terms before you buy.

Which one lists more lessons and tools?

The Devotion System does. Its page lists an ebook, 13 videos, a three-part quiz, and three bonus books. His Secret Obsession explains its Hero Instinct and Secret Signals ideas, but its full lesson list is not as clear.

Can either program make a man commit?

No. A course cannot control another person's feelings, choices, or actions. The seller pages make strong promises. We treat those as sales claims, not proven results.

Are these programs a replacement for therapy?

No. These are self-help products. They are not therapy, medical care, crisis help, or couples counseling. If you feel unsafe, get help from a qualified local service instead.

What should I check before I buy?

Check the price, what you get, the refund time, the help email, the privacy rules, and the billing terms. Make sure you know if the order is one payment or if it offers more products.

How long is the refund period?

Both seller pages checked on August 1, 2026 say they offer a 60-day money-back guarantee. Read the current order and refund rules before you pay.
